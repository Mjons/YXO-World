Questions?

Good to know.

What should my teen bring?

A laptop and charger. Everything else is provided.

Is this right for all teens 13 to 17?

Yes. The workshop is designed to engage teens across this age range, with activities that adapt to different maturity levels.

What if my teen is shy or introverted?

Our facilitators are experienced at creating a welcoming environment. Activities are designed to include everyone comfortably.

Can parents attend?

The workshop is teens only, so they can engage freely. Parents are welcome at the closing ceremony.
