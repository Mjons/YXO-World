3How we use it

  • Register your teen, hold their seat, and match the registration to the payment
  • Keep your teen safe, including planning food around allergies and reaching you in an emergency
  • Send confirmations, venue details, reminders and updates about the cohort
  • Process refunds, credits and seat transfers
  • Answer your questions and requests to bring YXO to your city
  • Tell waitlist members about new cohorts and early registration (you can opt out at any time)
  • Understand which pages and channels bring people to YXO, and measure our ads, so we can improve the site and our outreach
  • Meet legal, tax and accounting duties, and protect our rights

We use allergy and dietary details only for safety and catering. We do not use them for marketing.

4Teens' information

YXO serves teens ages 13 to 17, but we collect their information from their parent or guardian, not from the teens themselves. The website and its forms are meant for adults.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from anyone under 13. If we learn that a child under 13 has sent us information, we will delete it. If you think this has happened, email yxoworld@gmail.com.

Photos and video. We take photos and video at workshops. We use images in which your teen can be identified only if you tick the photo-consent box at registration, and we never post a teen's full name or school with their image. You can withdraw consent at any time by email.

During workshops, teens may use third-party AI and web tools under supervision. We teach them not to enter personal details such as full names, addresses or school names into those tools. Those providers' own privacy policies apply to what is entered there.

A parent or guardian can ask to see, correct or delete their teen's information at any time (section 8).

6Cookies, analytics and ads

Analytics. Our analytics tool, Plausible, works without cookies and reports only totals, such as how many people visited a page or which cities waitlist members are from.

Advertising. We advertise on Instagram and Facebook, and yxo.world uses the Meta Pixel to measure those ads. The pixel is a small piece of Meta's code that runs on every page of the site. It sets cookies in your browser (named _fbp and, if you arrive from one of our ads, _fbc) and sends Meta the pages you visit, the actions you take (viewing a page, joining the waitlist, requesting a workshop, going to checkout, and completing a payment, with the package price), and technical details such as your IP address, browser and device. If you are logged in to Facebook or Instagram, Meta can link this to your account. Meta uses it to report how our ads perform and to show our ads to people more likely to be interested, under its own privacy policy. We do not send Meta your name, your email address, anything you type into our forms, or any of your teen's details.

Your options. You can limit this in your Facebook or Instagram settings, under "Your activity off Meta technologies" and in your ad preferences, or block the pixel with your browser's tracking protection, a content blocker, or private browsing. The site works the same either way. Like most sites, yxo.world does not respond to browser "Do Not Track" signals.

Stripe's checkout page may use its own cookies for security and fraud prevention.

7How long we keep it, and security

We keep information only as long as we need it:

Information How long we keep it
Allergy and dietary details Deleted within 90 days after the cohort ends
Registration details (your teen's name, date of birth, grade and school, and your contact details) 2 years after the cohort, so we can answer questions and invite you back
Payment and refund records 7 years, as tax and accounting rules require
Waitlist and workshop-request entries Until you ask us to stop writing to you or to delete them
Photos and video As long as we use them, or until you withdraw consent

We limit access to the small team that runs each cohort, use providers with industry-standard security, and never store full card numbers. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your information, we will tell you as the law requires.

8Your choices and rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • Show you the information we hold about you or your teen
  • Correct anything that is wrong or out of date
  • Delete it, unless we must keep it for legal or accounting reasons
  • Stop sending you waitlist or marketing emails (reply "stop" to any of them, or write to us)
  • Stop using your teen's photos or video in new materials

Email yxoworld@gmail.com from the address you used to register. We will confirm who you are and reply within 30 days. We will not treat you differently for making a request.

Residents of some states, such as California, have extra rights under their state laws. We will honor those requests as those laws require.
