Lily Safrani.

Founder, YXOYoung Extraordinary Odyssey

Lily Safrani is a serial entrepreneur, the Founder and CEO of ExO Speakers, and the founder of YXO.

For 15 years, she's had a seat in the rooms where the future is discussed, from the beginning of Singularity University to XPRIZE and Abundance360.

As a mom, she saw how much those rooms had to offer teens, and how few teens ever got in. YXO is her answer: workshops that put creativity first and AI second, so teens can shape their future now, not after college.

Natasha Tsakos.

Tech MentorAward-winning director & producer

An award-winning director, performer and producer pioneering the intersection of arts, technology, innovation and space exploration.

Space & innovation

Founder of SPACE WONDERS, the first theatrical company for space, developing experiences born from the medium of space and felt on Earth. Her flagship production, Paraboles, is the first research-driven performance in microgravity and a cultural prototype for life beyond Earth.

Global impact

She has captivated audiences across six continents, from TED and the Super Bowl with Cirque du Soleil to the G20 Summit, Discovery Channel and Tribeca Film Festival. She speaks at the United Nations General Assembly and Google.

Strategic partnerships

Drawing on work with NASA, MIT and the International Institute for Astronautical Sciences, Natasha brings a research-driven approach to artistic innovation.

Singularity University

A graduate of SU’s Global Solutions Program (2015) at NASA Research Park, and winner of SU’s Global Impact Competition, sponsored by Google and NASA.

Youth engagement

Years spent turning “showmaking” into a practical toolkit for young creators: workshops in storytelling, experiential design and creativity with technology, from Telemundo’s Protagonista de la Novela to DASH, the Art Directors Club and Miami Animation & Gaming International Complex. Most recently she sparked a room of 300 eleven-year-old girls at Space Center Houston.

Education

BFA in Theatre (New World School of the Arts / University of Florida), plus a NASA certificate of training (Chamber Flight to FL250), MIT’s New Space Economy program, a NASA L’SPACE certificate, and studies with the IIAS.

Her mission

To create extraordinary experiences, on this planet and beyond, that inspire wonder and awaken new ways of being.
