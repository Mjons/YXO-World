2The workshop

YXO is a full-day, in-person workshop for teens ages 13 to 17 on AI, entrepreneurship and purpose. The pilot cohort is held on Nov 7, 2026, 10 AM to 4 PM, at Brickell Mater Academy, 230 SW 17th Rd, Miami, FL 33129. Brickell Mater Academy is providing the space and is not the organizer of YXO. 3Registration and payment

Only a parent or guardian may register a teen. Please give us accurate information, including allergies, medical needs and an emergency contact, and tell us if anything changes. A seat is confirmed once payment is complete.

The price is $300 for one teen or $500 for two. Payment is handled by Stripe, and your statement may show "Graceworks." We never see your full card number.

Refunds. Cancel 14 or more days before the workshop for a full refund, less Stripe's fee. Cancel 7 to 13 days before for a 50% refund or a full credit toward a future cohort, your choice. Within 7 days, or if your teen does not attend, no refund is owed. You can transfer the seat to another eligible teen at no cost. To cancel, email lily@exospeakers.com. Questions about a charge? Email us first and we will sort it out quickly.

4Safety and supervision

All YXO facilitators are screened before working with teens. At least two adults are present at all times, and staff do not spend time alone with a single teen or message teens privately.

Teens stay on campus from drop-off to pickup, including lunch. Phones stay put away during the day. To reach your teen, call or text 1-646-522-1414. We release teens only to you or an adult you name, unless you tell us in writing that your teen may leave on their own.

5Health and emergencies

Teens who need emergency medication, such as an EpiPen or inhaler, should carry it themselves. Staff do not give medication. We take reasonable care with food but cannot guarantee an allergen-free environment.

If your teen is hurt or ill, we will contact you right away. If we cannot reach you or your emergency contact, you authorize us to call emergency services and consent to emergency medical care for your teen. You are responsible for the cost of that care.

You give permission for your teen to use them under our supervision. We teach teens not to enter personal information into these tools.
